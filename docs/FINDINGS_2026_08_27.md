## 2. The target changed: attribution replaces confounding

**The student's redesign, 26 August.** A bidirected edge says "something unobserved links
these two" and stops. The target is now a **latent group** — the clique of window variables
one hidden cause explains, plus the AGENT whose private block holds it. Attribution
REPLACES the confounding claim rather than joining it, so an agent cannot bank the easy half
while failing the half that carries the thesis.

Three facts established while formalising it (`cb/attribution.py`):

**Every bidirected edge in a window joins two SHARED variables, and its latent lies in
exactly one agent's block.** The confounding path runs entirely through hidden variables,
those live in private blocks, and cross-block edges are forbidden — so the path stays inside
one block and exits to something that block's owner can see. Attribution is therefore a
choice among NAMED agents, not a search over arbitrary structures.

**Multi-owner pairs are common enough to model**: 12–25% of confounded pairs have two
owners, over 250 graphs and several topologies. A single-owner simplification would be
wrong a fifth of the time.

**The hypothesis space had to be coarsened, and this was a correctness fix.** "One latent
parenting {u,v,w}" and "three pairwise latents in the same block" are indistinguishable by
ANY available evidence — a partner action moving all three is consistent with both, and no
partial response is possible because the agent is told which PARTNER acted, never which
variable. The finer hypothesis therefore survives forever and the claim can never settle.
Groups are canonicalised to MAXIMAL CLIQUES per owner, which deletes exactly the undecidable
refinements: `{u,v}` and `{v,w}` with `u-w` NOT confounded stay separate, and a partial
response tells them apart.

### Altruism was not expressible before this

Found while building it, and it reframes every earlier deterministic number. The
version-space belief prunes on the agent's OWN window columns (`cb/versionspace.py:310`),
and a partner's private variable is not a window column. **A partner's private experiment
was invisible.** Not "under-rewarded" — invisible. So the 0.937 headline is a statement
about dividing shared work and contains no altruism component, and could not have.

### The behaviour attribution demands

3 agents x 2 private + 3 shared, scale-free, budget 12, fixed policies, 40 episodes:

| policy | structure | attribution | identified |
|---|---|---|---|
| shared only | 0.864 | **0.000** | 0.000 |
| private only | 0.891 | **0.981** | 0.292 |
| probe then work | 0.926 | 0.907 | **0.658** |
| random | 0.861 | 0.407 | 0.275 |

Either pure strategy lands near 0.29; the mixture more than doubles it. A private probe pays
only the PARTNERS, so this is a coordination problem in the strict sense — the group-optimal
behaviour is one no selfish agent has a reason to adopt.

**And the learners find it.** Three seeds, 3200 episodes, 150 evaluation episodes, all arms
on identical episodes: learned **0.670 ± 0.005** against the hand-designed **0.667 ± 0.034**
and greedy's 0.582. They MATCH the strategy derived from theory without being told it, under
a per-agent reward. The honest phrasing is "found a strategy as good as the one theory
predicts", not "beat the baseline".

Not yet supported: that individual agents are altruistic. A private probe also orients the
prober's own private-incident edges, so budget share does not isolate the motive. The
system-level claim is safe; the individual one needs the reward ablation.

## 3. Transfer fails, and we know the mechanism

4 agents x 1 private, budget 8, 60 episodes:

| environment | arm | identified | duplicate coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| deterministic | learned s0/s1/s2 | 0.988 / 0.967 / 0.954 | 0.041 / 0.076 / 0.072 |
| deterministic | greedy | 0.729 | 0.286 |
| statistical | **transferred** | **0.171** | 0.116 |
| statistical | greedy | 0.229 | 0.447 |
| statistical | random | 0.208 | 0.504 |

It loses to RANDOM. A policy merely confused by unfamiliar inputs drifts TOWARD random; this
went below it, so it was executing a trained strategy that is wrong there.

**What it actually does**, moved to the noisy environment: spends more of its budget on its
own private variable (0.55 → **0.69**), touches fewer distinct shared variables (0.69 →
**0.55**), and repeats itself five times as often (0.12 → **0.56**). With two moves each,
that means both moves on its own variable in over half of episodes. **It stops
participating in the shared work.**

**The mechanism, demonstrated rather than argued.** Blur only what the policy SEES, inside
the environment it has mastered — same task, same reward, same exact belief underneath:

| blur | private share | distinct shared | repeats | identified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | 0.558 | 0.68 | 0.09 | **0.988** |
| 0.50 | 0.635 | 0.61 | 0.22 | 0.929 |
| 0.75 | **0.727** | **0.53** | **0.48** | 0.633 |
| 1.00 | 0.744 | 0.49 | 0.55 | 0.412 |
| *(transferred, for comparison)* | *0.691* | *0.55* | *0.56* | *0.171* |

Blur 0.75 reproduces the transfer profile almost exactly. In the deterministic environment
every belief is all-or-nothing; in the statistical one they are smeared across the middle.
The policy has never seen a half-settled belief and falls back on the move that was always
safe.

Caveat on the instrument: the blur SHRINKS frequencies toward 0.5, so it removes information
monotonically rather than corrupting it. It shows that information loss reproduces the
profile; it does not separate "sees less" from "sees differently".

## 4. The fix: one environment family with a noise dial

**The student's design.** The two environments differed in two ways at once — the data
(unlimited vs finite) and the machinery (crossing off candidates vs bootstrapped structure
search). Separate them: keep the fast machinery, feed it real samples. Where the belief
asked the true graph "is x an ancestor of y", it now asks the DATA.

**Two-directional but POWER-AWARE**, and the second half was a correctness fix found in
validation. Pruning only on positive evidence is sound but does not converge: a candidate
that OVER-claims an effect is never eliminated — 4.48 survivors at 4000 rows against the
oracle's 1.40, and flat in the row count. Undetected ancestry now refutes a candidate where
the pair was POWERED.

**That also handles effect RANGE**, which the oracle ignored entirely — it answered ancestry
exactly at any distance, so a five-hop ancestor was as informative as a direct parent, which
changes which experiment is WORTH RUNNING and not merely what the agent sees. A distant
effect multiplies coefficients and accumulates noise, so it is under-powered by nature and
its candidates survive. No hand-coded decay. (Student's observation.)

Validated, 30 episodes per cell, identical action sequences in both engines:

| rows per experiment | sampled survivors | oracle survivors | superset of oracle | truth kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 1.91 | 1.40 | 0.956 | 0.956 |
| 1000 | 1.46 | 1.40 | 0.978 | 0.978 |
| 4000 | **1.41** | 1.40 | 0.978 | 0.978 |

**The evidence threshold is non-monotone and 1e-3 is the optimum**, with an empty-space
guard raising truth retention to **0.992**:

| alpha | truth kept | survivors | identified |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-2 | 0.917 | 2.85 | 0.583 |
| **1e-3** | **0.992** | 1.97 | **0.717** |
| 1e-4 | 0.967 | 1.73 | 0.692 |
| 1e-6 | 0.967 | 1.74 | 0.683 |

Stricter is NOT safer: loosening lets false detections kill the truth, tightening lets real
effects go undetected while the power test still says they should have shown, so the truth
is pruned for OVER-claiming instead. Two failure modes pulling opposite ways.

**The price, stated plainly.** "Settled implies correct" now holds up to 0.8% rather than
absolutely. That was the deterministic environment's defining property and it has been
traded deliberately for realism and a dial.

## 5. Everything I got wrong, and how it was caught

Six withdrawals in two days. Four share one cause: reporting a difference smaller than its
own noise.

1. **"Partner memory is worth +0.20, three seeds out of three."** Read off single 16-episode
   batches, quantised to 1/64. Smoothed, the gap swings between −0.04 and +0.17. The
   supportable claim is that it makes learning more CONSISTENT (seed spread ±0.009 against
   ±0.043), not better.
2. **Three agenda items reported as "measured worse"** (foreign rows, skeleton threshold,
   mixed action modes). All at 30–40 episodes, all differences inside ~1.5 standard errors.
   Correct wording is "no evidence of benefit".
3. **"The engine discards the altruistic clamp."** A mechanism asserted in prose before
   being tested, then refuted by the test: telling a partner HELPS, 5x under clamp and 18x
   under vary.
4. **"The coordination transfers intact."** Wrong, and the metric misled me. Duplicate
   coverage is wasted shared work as a fraction of shared work ATTEMPTED, so a policy that
   barely attempts any scores near zero by default. Low duplication meant not showing up.
5. **"The idealisation teaches agents not to repeat, and repeating buys power."** Refuted by
   the fixed-policy sweep: dividing the work is better in BOTH environments with identical
   ordering (divided 1.000/0.245, random 0.350/0.095, converged 0.130/0.085). This one
   nearly cost a day of building the wrong fix.
6. **`probe_then_work` counted queries, not moves.** Under turn-taking the policy is queried
   every round and the inactive agent's move discarded, so its probe phase expired before it
   acted once — 0.000 attribution against 0.907 for the identical policy standalone. It made
   the learners look like they beat the reference when the comparison had not been run.

Two process changes follow, and they are already in the handover: every comparison paired on
identical episodes with the per-episode difference reported, and nothing reported below 150
episodes.

## 6. What is not yet supported

- **Individual altruism.** Private probes serve the prober too. Needs the reward ablation.
- **Attribution above 3 agents.** Nothing has been run.
- **Attribution's truth retention under sampled evidence.** The structure channel is
  validated at 99.2%; the attribution channel is not. An early 12-episode check had sampled
  attribution ABOVE oracle, which is the wrong direction and suggests the estimated
  "moved" set may over-fire.
- **That the noise dial fixes transfer.** Training at three noise levels is running.

## 7. Scale: the environment now reaches 30-variable windows

**The blocker was not the belief.** `ma/projection.py::latent_projection` — the GROUND TRUTH
computation, used to grade the agents — searched every conditioning subset, 2^(k-2) per pair.
At k=15 that is 105 pairs x 8,192 subsets. Profiling put the cost there, not in the
inference. Replaced with Verma & Pearl's inducing-path characterisation, searched as
reachability over (node, arrived-by-arrowhead) states. **Validated at 1440/1440 exact
agreement** with the subset-search implementation, across two generators and four topologies.

**The belief change (student's design).** `cb/factored.py` holds one small version space PER
PAIR rather than an explicit set of whole-window structures. Viable because the
interventional evidence is already pairwise: on an adjacent pair, ancestry from x to y admits
only x -> y and its absence admits only {y -> x, x <-> y}, so intervening on both endpoints
determines the mark with no joint reasoning. 4*C(k,2) numbers — 1,740 at k=30 against 10^19
candidates.

| window k | nodes | enumerated ms/ep | factored ms/ep | pairs settled | truth kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 0.878 | 1.000 |
| 6 | 12 | 882 | 8 | 0.869 | 1.000 |
| 8 | 16 | GAVE UP | 15 | 0.923 | 1.000 |
| 15 | 29 | GAVE UP | 56 | 0.798 | 1.000 |
| 20 | 40 | GAVE UP | 121 | 0.778 | 1.000 |
| **30** | **60** | **GAVE UP** | **349** | **0.824** | **1.000** |

And it TRAINS there: 3 agents with 30-variable windows over 54 nodes at 508 ms an episode,
6 agents with 15-variable windows over 40 nodes at 400 ms — on the portable policy, with no
architecture change, because that half was already scale-agnostic.

**It is a RELAXATION, not a different answer.** Both engines on identical episodes and
identical action sequences:

| window k | pairs | enumeration settled | factored settled | not a relaxation | contradictions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 720 | 1.000 | 1.000 | 0 | **0** |
| 5 | 1200 | 0.900 | 0.867 | 0 | **0** |

Coarser at k=5, exactly as designed, and never settling a pair differently.

**The price.** No joint constraints, so it stays unsure where the enumeration would have
settled. And the exact ceiling and exact optimal-rounds figures enumerate reachable
intervention sets, so they are unavailable above k=6: **exact headroom at small scale,
honest bounds above.** Write it that way.

**Not done:** no TRAINED results above k=6 yet. This is the environment being ready, not the
experiment being run.

## 8. The noise dial is INCONCLUSIVE — do not report it

| arm | learned | greedy | random |
|---|---|---|---|
| oracle, n_int 20 | 0.442 | 0.583 | 0.108 |
| sampled, n_int 100 | 0.100 | 0.292 | 0.100 |
| sampled, n_int 1000 | 0.233 | 0.467 | 0.100 |

The learners lose to greedy in all three, contradicting the 4-agent result (0.937 vs 0.405).
**Two things changed at once**: scale-free graphs instead of Erdos-Renyi, AND 3,000 episodes
instead of 20,000. Unattributable as it stands.

The learning curves favour undertrained — the oracle arm was still climbing steeply when it
stopped (0.398 -> 0.441 -> 0.508 -> 0.688 across quarters, entropy still 1.06) — but that is
a reading, not a measurement.

**This also puts a caveat on the headline result**, which has only ever been measured on
Erdos-Renyi. Whether it is generator-independent is now an open question, and resolving it
is job 5 in the handover.
