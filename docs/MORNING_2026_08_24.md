## Decisions taken under the blanket authorization (each logged with numbers)

1. **Vary-mode adopted for constraint arms** ("measure, then pick", as agreed): mean
   credit 0.60/0.51 vs clamp's 0.50/0.32 over 12 known-graph episodes. The engine gained
   a first-order channel: within intervened rows x's values are exogenous, so corr(x, y)
   is causation read directly; matching Fisher-z power branch. Both inert on clamp data.
2. **alpha stays 0.01** — the sanctioned one-time sweep: 0.05 halves credit (noisier
   skeletons). Fixed; do not revisit against results.
3. **identify_threshold left at 0.7, UNCHANGED** — binary identification is rare (see
   cross-check below) and the threshold was set for posterior mass, not replicate
   fractions, but recalibrating it is your call, not mine.
4. **Round-robin for constraint arms** — simultaneous interventions starve the engine's
   two-sample contrasts (a known third-variable clamp excludes the row).
5. **disclose_regime ON for the overnight arms** — the foreign-row mask consumes the
   regime bit; the no-bit arm is the attribution study, not the capability demonstration.
6. **potential_shaping 0.1** — policy-invariant (Ng et al.), and the smoke showed the
   sparse reward is sampled early (solve 0.31 in the first random-ish update).

## Bugs found and fixed overnight (6, 7, 8 — the count is now eight)

- **Bug 6** — another agent's PRIVATE clamp contaminates ancestry contrasts; the window's
  own mask cannot flag it. Agent B sat at credit 0.00 on every seed, its engine
  attributing A's hidden clamp to inert variables. Fix: per-row `foreign` mask in
  `FisherZ`, derived from the regime bit under the existing disclosure gate.
  **Second form:** `clean` counts clamps only, so a hidden VARY evaded the mask —
  the env now tracks mode-agnostic `hidden_intervened`, and `_refresh` hands each
  backend the summary it needs (clamped fraction vs regime flag).
- **Bug 7** — the per-pair power anchor r was computed on regime-POOLED rows; a clamp
  upstream of a confounded pair severs the dependence being measured (true r 0.70 read
  as 0.345 → power declared absent → detectable confounder reported undetermined).
  Anchor now comes from pure-regime rows only. Found by the cross-check, exactly as the
  plan predicted.
- **Bug 8** — `evaluate_episode` would have indexed `space.dags[-1]` (the LAST DAG) for
  the constraint backend's MAP-less report. Caught by reading, not by failing. The
  constraint union is majority-vote directed edges now.

## The cross-check, honestly (the decision that still belongs to you)

Exact vs constraint on 12 identical seeded episodes, k=4, after all fixes: exact
identifies 12/12 (mass 0.78–0.98), constraint mean credit 0.5–0.6 with binary
identification rare. **The divergence is one-directional: the constraint engine
under-claims, never wrongly claims — zero false identifications, zero false confounders
across every probe all night.** The residual gap is the clamp-to-0 variance channel's
intrinsic power limit (needs |r| ≳ 0.5 at our block sizes; the exact engine reads
first-order likelihood and handles |r| ≈ 0.25). Vary-mode narrows it; data volume alone
does not (measured flat 250→4000 rows/clamp pre-fix).

Open question for you: whether 0.7-on-replicate-fraction is the right identification
bar, or whether the constraint criterion should be recalibrated (e.g. majority). I did
not touch it.
