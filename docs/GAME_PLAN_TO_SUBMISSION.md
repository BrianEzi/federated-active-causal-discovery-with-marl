## What is already bankable, and it is a lot

These are settled, measured, and independent of everything still running:

1. **Forced-cover characterisation** — 0.757k at k=4 to 0.542k at k=30, closed form, used
   predictively to set budgets.
2. **Budget starvation** — the window ladder gave k=30 a smaller budget than k=20; greedy
   goes 0.000 -> 0.760 when normalised. Retires the old scaling story.
3. **Credit assignment** — 75% of every agent's training rows were discarded actions carrying
   another agent's reward. Fixing it: MI 0.425 -> 0.700.
4. **FedAvg equals pooling per update** — 0.9971 cosine, 0.99x displacement, measured on
   weights. The gap is discarded optimiser state, which is the known FedAdam problem.
5. **Rescue does not exist on this backend**, because the skeleton is oracle-seeded. Answers
   "why not clamp?" outright.
6. **The skeleton assumption, measured** — recoverable in principle (4,710/4,710 pairs) and
   worth essentially all identification at the operating sample size.
7. **Attribution to k=12** — 18 of 20 sweep cells, crosschecked, zero wrong.
8. **Graph-distribution confounds** — the confounded filter discarding 72% at k=4 and 0% at
   k=30.
9. **n_int is the binding axis under sampled evidence**, not n_obs.
10. **Decentralisation costs the learned advantage** — solo matches greedy; sharing is what
    beats it.

That is a thesis. The runs still going make it stronger; they are not what makes it exist.
