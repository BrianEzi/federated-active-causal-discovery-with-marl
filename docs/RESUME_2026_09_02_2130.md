## 2. WHAT IS ESTABLISHED

### RQ1 — the sweep
* **k=20 headline, six seeds:** learned SHD 0.00000 against myopic 0.00051. Five of six seeds
  commit zero errors over 200 episodes each; six of six paired differences significant.
  Joint recovery 0.9900 +/- 0.0100 against 0.8958.
* **At 12,000 episodes the learned policy beats the myopic rule at every window size**, every
  agent count and every budget tested. 14 of 15 measured cells favour it; the exception is a
  checkpoint artefact.
* **The only surviving axis effect is contention.** Grouped by contended fraction: sigma=0.25
  median ratio 0.02, sigma=0.50 median 0.45, sigma=0.75 ratio 0.89. The margin narrows as more
  of each window is contested. It does not reverse.
* **Mechanism for that:** shared-shared pairs are solved by every competent policy (0.00000 for
  both learned and myopic over 90,000 pair observations). Contention costs the learned policy
  accuracy on its own PRIVATE variables (private-incident error 0.00015 at four agents to
  0.00109 at eight) while the myopic rule's falls.

### RQ2 — transfer (agent B)
* Seven answer rates, three seeds, 200 paired episodes. **12 of 12 seeds at rho <= 0.90 beat
  greedy; 0 of 6 at rho >= 0.95 do.** Spread 11.3x the seed SE. Verdict DOSE-RESPONSE SUPPORTED,
  fired only after a guard that withheld twice.
* Monotone through rho=0.50, which is the best point at -0.01785 and has the WORST distribution
  match. **Calibration is refuted** by a prediction made in advance.
* **Mechanism measured:** a fixed rho=1.00 policy evaluated under withholding degrades from
  -0.00035 to +0.04938 -- FASTER than greedy. Training adaptation is worth 0.055, about twice
  the transfer curve's whole spread. The unadapted policy's variance grows 240x; the adapted
  policy's stays flat.
* Scope note to carry: the decomposition varies withholding under ORACLE evidence. Adaptation to
  withholding is measured; that the same adaptation carries to finite-sample evidence is
  inferred.

### RQ3 — the price of federation
* Ladder arms A (federated) and E (information and optimiser partitions removed), six seeds:
  federated 0.00020, centralised 0.00037, paired difference **-0.00017 +/- 0.00023, inside one
  SE of zero.** Mean and median disagree on direction. No measurable cost.
* Action rights stay partitioned in both, so this is the cost of partitioning information,
  reward and optimisation -- not decentralisation entire.
* **Coordination baselines are the stronger result:** a communication-free positional convention
  scores 0.745 at k=12 and **0.487** at k=20, against uncoordinated myopic 0.927 and 0.897.
  Dividing the contested surface by convention is worse than leaving it contested.

### RQ4 — attribution (SCOPED DOWN, see section 4)
* **5,224 correct, 0 incorrect, 19,727 groups, 28 cells, window sizes 6 to 50.** The engine
  names an owner or abstains; it has never named the wrong one.
* Reach predicted in closed form: `0.76 x (share of latents spanning exactly two variables)`,
  largest residual **0.041**. Computable from topology with no simulation.
* Coverage is a step function: 21/1056 at budget 30; **349/1056 at 60, 120 and 240** -- the

## 5. THE TWO CONVENTIONS THAT GOVERN EVERY NUMBER

1. **Checkpoint.** The reported policy is the checkpoint maximising training-rollout mutual
   information. At 12,000 episodes this and the final update disagree by more than 3x on
   **8 of 21 seeds, in BOTH directions** (5 selected-worse, 3 final-worse). Neither is safe
   alone, so the chapter reports both columns. `FINDINGS_CHECKPOINT_TAIL_2026_09_02.md`.
2. **Action selection.** Sampling at temperature 1, never argmax. On a good policy the two agree
   to within 0.0002; a large gap is a symptom of a weak policy.

**A result file's own `global_hard_shd` is NOT what the thesis reports.** It is that run's
evaluation at its final update, and on a long run the two differ by up to 300x on the same seed.
Three separate errors came from reading it as though it were. Always
`scripts/global_shd_paired.py --checkpoint best --sample`.
