Withdrawn · 20 August 2026

This report has been withdrawn

It reported two-agent training results scored with a success metric that could not be earned on confounded episodes — the case the two-agent design exists to study. On those episodes the reported rate was structurally 0.000, so every headline figure in it was silently conditioned on the episodes that need no coordination.

What that invalidates:

  • the with-bit and no-bit success rates, and the paired comparison against random;
  • any statement in it about how the two arms compare.

The fault was found and fixed on the morning of 20 August, the affected runs were re-done, and the corrected results — along with a full account of the bug and the test that now guards against it — are in the current briefing:
